Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Forsko (accessible from https://www.forsko.in/). Protecting your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse free academic resources (Syllabus, PYQs, SGPA Calculator) without creating an account. If you voluntarily contact us via email, WhatsApp, or submit study materials through our contributor form, we may collect your name, email address, or submitted academic files.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like most websites, Forsko uses standard log files and analytics tools to understand visitor traffic (such as browser type, device, pages visited, and timestamps). This data is used solely to improve website performance and content.

3. Cookies & Google AdSense

Forsko may use third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense to serve advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to Forsko and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Third-Party Links & Payments

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Drive, or payment gateways for premium study materials). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

5. Children's Information

Forsko is an educational platform intended for college and university students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Contact Us
